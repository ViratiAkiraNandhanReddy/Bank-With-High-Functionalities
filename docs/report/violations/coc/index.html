Community / Code of Conduct

Build with care.
Leave room for others.

A shared standard for constructive discussion, thoughtful reviews, and welcoming contribution.

ON THIS PAGEStandardExpectedUnacceptableEnforcement
01 / OUR STANDARD

Make contribution feel possible.

Participation should be a positive, respectful, and learning-oriented experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, identity, or communication style.

Assume good intent.Ask questions before assigning motives, explain the impact of a concern, and leave the codebase clearer than you found it.
02 / EXPECTED

What good collaboration looks like.

Be respectfulUse welcoming language and treat differing technical opinions as part of healthy review.
Be specificInclude context, reproduction steps, and the smallest useful example.
Be curiousExplain unfamiliar choices, document tradeoffs, and help new contributors.
Be accountableAccept feedback, correct mistakes, and avoid moving unresolved problems downstream.
03 / BOUNDARIES

What is not welcome.

HarassmentPersonal attacks, discriminatory comments, intimidation, or unwanted contact.
Bad-faith participationDeliberate disruption, trolling, impersonation, or repeatedly ignoring guidance.
Privacy violationsSharing credentials, personal information, or private reports without permission.
Unsafe disclosurePublishing vulnerability details before maintainers have a reasonable chance to respond.
04 / RESPONSE

Raise concerns privately.

Report conduct concerns through official private contact channels. Include context and what outcome would help. Reports are reviewed with discretion and may result in a warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces.

Good-faith reporting

Reports made honestly and respectfully will not result in retaliation. Urgent security vulnerabilities should use the dedicated security reporting process.
